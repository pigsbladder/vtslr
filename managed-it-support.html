IT Support FAQs

What Types of Businesses Do You Support?+

We primarily support small to midsize businesses across Arkansas that depend on computers, networks, internet access and business software every day. Many clients have roughly 10 to 50 workstations, with some larger environments as well. We act as an outsourced IT team for organizations that need dependable support without managing everything internally.

Do You Provide Remote IT Support?+

Yes. Many issues can be handled remotely using secure management tools, so technicians can troubleshoot PCs, servers and common user issues without waiting for a visit. When a problem needs hands-on work, we provide on-site help.

Do You Monitor Networks After Hours?+

Yes. We provide 24/7/365 network monitoring to help identify outages and warning signs that need attention. In-house support is available Monday through Friday, 8AM to 5PM Central.

How Fast Can We Expect a Response?+

We offer a five-minute-or-less response commitment for support requests and a one-hour-or-less emergency response commitment. Needs vary by issue and urgency, but the model is built around quick communication so interruptions do not sit unresolved.

Can You Help with More Than Basic Troubleshooting?+

Yes. Support can include network support, cloud computing, cybersecurity, backup and recovery, vendor coordination, hardware and software management and maintenance renewals. Many technology problems involve more than one system or vendor, and we connect those details so your team is not managing every moving part.

How Is Pricing Handled?+

Our managed services and cloud services are flat-rate fee offerings, with packages built around your business size, environment and budget. A conversation about your current setup is the best way to define the right scope.
