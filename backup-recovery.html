Backup & Recovery FAQs

What Do Backup and Disaster Recovery Include?+

Backup protects copies of your important files, systems and business data. Disaster recovery is the plan and process for getting systems usable again after an outage, hardware failure, cyber incident or other disruption. We manage both so backup protection connects to practical recovery planning.

How Do I Know My Backups Are Working?+

A backup should not be assumed reliable because it was set up once. Backup jobs need monitoring, review and follow-up when failures or warning signs appear. Our proactive monitoring means problems are more likely to be addressed before they become recovery problems.

Can You Help If We Already Have a Backup System?+

Yes. We can review your current backup and recovery setup as part of a broader IT conversation. Many businesses have some backup in place but lack clear visibility, recovery expectations or vendor coordination. We help identify practical next steps without burying your team in technical language.

How Quickly Can We Recover After Data Loss?+

Recovery time depends on the systems affected, the backup method, the amount of data and the condition of the environment. We do not promise one timeline for every situation. Our role is to help you plan ahead, reduce preventable delays and respond quickly when a problem happens.

If you are unsure whether your backups would hold up after an attack, failure or mistake, we can review your current setup and map the practical next steps.
